a little about me

I grew up in Chennai, where nobody speaks just one language — so naturally my undergrad thesis taught a model to understand Tamil-English code-mixed text, sarcasm included.

Now I'm doing my M.S. in Data Science at UMD, working on NLP that catches the exact moment a support chat turns sour, and building frontend at Venusgeo by day.

I like the space where analysis meets design: the model is only half the job, the other half is making someone care about what it found.
